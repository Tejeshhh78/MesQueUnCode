Mes Que Un Code

Twenty-two players, one ball, 199,319 frames.
We read the match like a rondo: who has it, who hunts it, and when it truly changes hands.

A possession model that thinks in spells, not frames: it reads automatic tracking, tactical tagging and team shape, then decodes the match into whole possessions with a three-state Viterbi decoder.

El repte · The challenge

For every frame of the match, say who has the ball.

One LaLiga match — FC Barcelona vs Athletic Club — recorded by a fixed tactical camera. The output is one label per video frame, scored against a private frame-by-frame ground truth.

199,319frames to label, at 25 fps
158,857frames actually graded (two halves)
41%of graded frames with no ball position
46anonymous track slots for 22 players

Ranked on spellF1, not accuracy

A possession is a maximal run of H or A. Predicted possessions are matched one-to-one with true ones by temporal IoU, averaged over thresholds 0.3–0.7. One flicker inside a long possession splits it in two: one hit lost, one false alarm gained.

Tactical vs broadcast

Row i of the submission is frame i of the tactical video. The broadcast feed has its own 164,488-frame timeline with cuts and replays and is not synchronised — a file with ~164k rows indexed the wrong video.

L'alfabet · The alphabet

Four labels, three states

H

Home in possession

Barcelona has controlled possession and the ball is alive. Loose balls stay with the last team that had it.

A

Away in possession

Athletic Club has controlled possession. A deflection or a lost aerial duel is not a change.

D

Dead ball

Out of play: throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, fouls, goals, before kick-off. Never anyone's possession.

X

No answer

Abstain. Free on ungraded frames; under spellF1 it repeats your previous label. We write it only outside the halves.

Com funciona · How it works

Tiki-taka for tensors: from tracking noise to whole possessions

Five stages, numpy and pandas only, about two seconds for the full match once the tracking is cached.

  1. Align every source to the tactical video

    ATD frame_id − 1, Smart Tagging round(seconds × 25), homography as-is. Graded halves come from games.half_video_blocks; the per-half IdFrame offsets never leak into the tracking join.

  2. Score who could be on the ball

    Nearest player per team with a Gaussian control kernel, discounted when the ball flies at pass speed. Short ball gaps are interpolated; long ones get zero evidence, so the current team is carried through.

  3. Score whether the ball is dead

    Ball past a line (and still out while unseen), walking players, a ball placed for a restart, set-piece tags — and every frame before a detected kick-off is forced to D.

  4. Decode spells, not frames

    A Viterbi decoder charges a price for every hand-over and every stoppage, so a change only happens when the new state's evidence pays for it. That is exact hysteresis.

  5. Clean up, write, validate

    Sub-second spells are absorbed, dead-ball blips inside one possession bridged, and the CSV is checked with the official validator before the run reports success.

El marcador · Measured, not promised

The scoreboard: baseline vs Mes Que Un Code

The rules say anything tuned must be tuned on external data, so that is where we measure too: Metrica's three public sample games, labels from their own events, tracking degraded to look like the competition's, scored with the official metric code. Each game is scored by a model tuned on the other two. Our full-match score on the real match is scored privately by the organisers.

Fair play · Playing by the rules

Data constraints and gated assets

Gated match data

Video, tracking, tagging and homography are licensed to enrolled teams only. They live in data/ or wherever MTAH_DATA_DIR points, and are gitignored. Without them the pipeline stops with setup instructions; the scorer still runs on the public CSVs.

Tuned on other matches only

Everything fitted or tuned uses external data: Metrica's public sample games. On the real match the model only applies those values — a test fails if anything is fitted there. No hand labels, no tuning on the ground truth, no video; the public 5 minutes only check that the pipeline is correct.

Noisy by design

No player identities, slots that swap and vanish, a ball missing on 41% of graded frames, automatic tags with errors. Every feature is collective and every missing value is an explicit “no information”.
